## Activation criteria

Define who may declare a disaster, what evidence is required, and which communication channel becomes authoritative.

## Recovery sequence

For every step include owner, prerequisite, command or procedure, expected result, validation, and fallback.

## Verification

Test authentication, primary workflows, data integrity, integrations, monitoring, alerting, backups, and security controls.

## Return to normal operations

Document synchronization, failback, stakeholder approval, enhanced monitoring, evidence retention, and the post-incident review.
